## Product Idea

CourseIQ helps students compare class difficulty and understand professor feedback before planning their schedule.

## MVP

### In

- Upload multiple text-based syllabus files in PDF, TXT, or DOCX format.
- Extract text into a common representation while preserving the filename and page number when available.
- Preserve meaningful headings and line boundaries for syllabus-aware recursive chunking.
- Retrieve relevant syllabus sections using semantic search with simple source citations.
- Estimate category-level weekly workload using grading weight, course level, credit hours, course type, assignment counts, and weekly frequency when available.
- Show absolute hours per week and each course's workload relative to the student's uploaded schedule.

### Frozen

- Reddit ingestion and workload evidence.
- Rate My Professors ingestion and professor-review evidence.
- Combining external review evidence with syllabus estimates.

These features remain part of the product direction, but they wait until syllabus ingestion and retrieval are reliable and testable.

### Parking Lot

- OCR for scanned PDFs and images.
- Additional document formats beyond PDF, TXT, and DOCX.
- Detailed citation spans beyond filename and page number.
- Hybrid semantic and exact-keyword retrieval.
- Week-by-week workload timelines based on individual deadlines.
- A genuinely iterative tool-calling RAG agent.

## Triage Decision

**Adopt the existing project and reclaim it while building forward.**

The product direction is coherent, the backend imports, and useful extraction and RAG starting points already exist. Rebuilding everything would discard working context without improving learning. However, existing files are not treated as understood merely because they run: the upload path, extraction code, workload engine, and borrowed RAG pipeline will be explained, tested, and revised as their features come due.
